Model assumptions and interpretation

Gross time-equivalent value = employees × 1,500 annual hours × eligible share × time-saving share × realisation share × £28/hour. Annual cost = fixed cost + per-employee cost × employees. Break-even realisation is cost divided by gross potential before realisation. The model excludes discounting, quality effects, uncertainty and general-equilibrium effects. Time savings are not necessarily cash savings, realised productivity or GDP gains.

03 / RESEARCH INTEGRITY

Designed for critical evaluation

Measurement

Exposure, observed adoption and realised outcomes are distinct constructs. Aggregate measures cannot establish firm-level behaviour.

Identification

Associational evidence is distinct from causal inference. Confounding, coverage and uncertainty require explicit treatment.

Reproducibility

The private research environment includes provenance documentation, validation tests and sensitivity analyses. Full replication materials may be shared with authorised reviewers under agreed conditions.

04 / ABOUT THE RESEARCHER

Dr Albert Amanollahnejad

Academic researcher and lecturer | Applied AI Economics, Econometrics & Quantitative Research

My research focuses on the economics of artificial intelligence, technological change, financial and labour markets, productivity and economic performance. I combine applied economic analysis, econometric modelling, quantitative research methods and statistical inference to investigate the economic implications of emerging technologies. This independent portfolio integrates empirical research, economic modelling and reproducible research engineering, with particular attention to measurement validity, uncertainty, robustness and the distinction between statistical association and causal inference.
